What Is the Campus Ambassador Program?

The InternBoot Campus Ambassador Program invites currently enrolled college and university students to represent InternBoot on their campuses and help fellow students discover and enroll in InternBoot's internship offerings. In return for driving verified intern registrations, Ambassadors are recognized with official program merchandise and become eligible for milestone-based monetary rewards.

The Program runs on a transparent, performance-based slab structure, with separate reward tiers for Ambassadors who refer Indian candidates and those who refer foreign or international candidates.

Who Can Become an Ambassador?

Eligibility

Open only to individuals currently enrolled in a recognised college or university.

Verification

Proof of college enrolment may be requested at any stage for verification purposes.

How the Program Works

1

Promote & Refer

Ambassadors promote InternBoot's internship programs to their peer network and refer candidates for registration.

2

Onboarding

A referral counts once the candidate has successfully registered, completed onboarding, and participated in the internship program.

3

Verification

InternBoot requires up to 10 business days to verify submitted candidate data after certificates are issued.

4

Reward Payout

Once verification is complete, the Ambassador receives the reward tied to the achieved slab.

Reward Structure

Rewards scale with the number of verified intern registrations an Ambassador delivers, and the slab thresholds and payouts differ depending on whether the referred candidates are Indian or foreign nationals.

Foreign Candidates

Foreign-candidate referrals carry higher payouts at every slab, and the top slab requires 2,500 verified registrations versus 1,500 for Indian candidates.

Ambassador Responsibilities

  • Submit accurate, authentic data for every referred candidate in the approved format.
  • Maintain discipline, professionalism, and integrity while representing InternBoot.
  • Monitor attendance and support retention of interns referred under their batch.
  • Avoid misinformation, fraud, or inappropriate conduct during promotion or data submission.
  • Falsified, manipulated, or duplicated data results in immediate disqualification and forfeiture of rewards.

Payment & Program Terms

  • Payments are transferred only to the bank details provided by the Ambassador after verification is complete.
  • InternBoot is not responsible for delays caused by inaccurate or incomplete banking information.
  • InternBoot reserves the right to modify, suspend, or terminate the Program at any time.
  • Participants will be notified of any major policy updates affecting eligibility or rewards.
