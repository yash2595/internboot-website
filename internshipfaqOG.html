1 What is InternBoot?Popular
InternBoot is a virtual internship platform that helps students and fresh graduates gain real-world industry experience through structured internship programs. It bridges the gap between classroom learning and corporate work environments through practical training and mentorship.
2 What is the internship structure?Popular
The internship has two phases:
• Phase 1: 15 days of live training (Mon–Thu, 2 hours/day, Fri for doubt-clearing)
• Phase 2: Live project phase with deadlines, focusing on industry-oriented work
3 Is InternBoot a remote or online internship program?
Yes. InternBoot offers 100% virtual internships, allowing students from different locations to participate remotely in training sessions, projects, and mentorship. You can join from anywhere with a stable internet connection.
4 Who can apply for InternBoot internships?
InternBoot internships are suitable for:
• College students (all years)
• University students
• Fresh graduates
• Early career professionals
No prior experience is required for most programs.
5 What is the duration of InternBoot internship programs?
InternBoot internship programs typically range from 45 days to 6 months, depending on program structure and university requirements. You can choose the duration that best fits your schedule and academic needs.
6 I couldn't submit the documentation form. What should I do?
If the form is closed or you're experiencing technical difficulties, please email your issue to info@internboot.com with the subject line "Documentation Form Issue". Our support team will review your case and provide further instructions within 24-48 hours.
7 Can I change my internship domain or duration?
Yes, change requests can be made via email to info@internboot.com before the program starts. Approval depends on seat availability and program guidelines. Please make requests as early as possible for better chances of accommodation.
8 Will the sessions be recorded?Popular
Yes, all training sessions are recorded and shared with interns. Recordings and learning materials will be accessible through our learning platform for those who miss live sessions or want to review the content.
9 Is there any payment required to join the internship?Popular
No. InternBoot internships are completely free of cost. There are no registration fees, training fees, or certificate charges. We believe in making quality internship opportunities accessible to everyone.
10 Do InternBoot interns receive a certificate?
Yes. Verified internship certificates are provided after successful completion of the program. Certificates include a unique verification code and can be validated through our official verification page. They are recognized by universities and employers.
11 When will I receive my certificate?
Certificates are issued after the successful completion of your internship and verification of all required tasks and activities.
12 Where can I download my certificate?
You can download your certificate from the "Download Certificate" section available inside the Student Portal after logging in with your registered credentials.
13 How do I access the Download Certificate section?
Log in to the Student Portal using your registered email ID and password. Once logged in, navigate to the "Download Certificate" section from the left-side menu.
14 Why is my certificate not available yet?
If your certificate is not yet available, it may still be under review or awaiting approval from the trainer/admin. Please check again later or contact support for assistance.
15 Is there any fee for downloading the certificate?
No. Eligible interns can download their certificates free of charge through the Student Portal.
16 What will be the structure of this Internship?
After receiving the Letter of Intent (LOI), interns get a pre-joining email containing:
• Link to InternBoot Discord channel for communication
• Orientation meeting link and schedule
• Detailed internship structure and guidelines
• Project details and deliverables
• Mentor assignment information
