What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your actions and preferences such as login details, language settings, and browsing preferences for a better user experience.

How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • To improve website functionality and performance
  • To understand how visitors interact with our website
  • To remember user preferences and settings
  • To provide a smoother and more personalized browsing experience
  • To support analytics and advertising services where applicable

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: These cookies are required for the website to function properly.
  • Analytics Cookies: These cookies help us measure traffic and understand user behavior on our site.
  • Preference Cookies: These cookies remember your settings and choices for a better experience.
  • Advertising Cookies: These may be used to show relevant advertisements and measure ad performance.

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect certain features and functionality of the website.
