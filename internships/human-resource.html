Human Resource Internship

Gain real-world HR experience and build the skills to launch your career in Human Resource Management.

Program Overview

Our HR Internship provides practical exposure to the core functions of Human Resource Management and how HR teams operate in real organizations. Interns will gain insights into recruitment, employee management, and modern HR practices.

What You Will Learn
Fundamentals of Human Resource Management
Recruitment and talent acquisition processes
Creating ATS-friendly resumes and resume screening
Sourcing candidates using LinkedIn and job portals
Interview preparation and selection techniques
Campus recruitment and college outreach strategies
Writing effective Job Descriptions (JDs)
Employee onboarding and orientation practices
Basics of HR policies and workplace guidelines
Payroll structure and CTC understanding
Introduction to labour laws and HR compliance
Performance appraisal and employee engagement
Use of HR technology and HRMS tools
Practical learning through case studies and mock interviews

Program Outcome

By the end of the internship, participants will develop a foundational understanding of HR operations and the skills required to begin a career in the HR domain.
