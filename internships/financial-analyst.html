Financial Analysis Internship

Master essential financial concepts in corporate finance, banking, investment analysis, and strategic financial decision-making.

Program Overview

Our Finance Internship is designed to give participants practical exposure to essential financial concepts used in corporate finance, banking, investment analysis, and financial decision-making. Interns will work with real-world financial data and develop analytical skills critical for the finance industry.

What You Will Learn
Capital structure and cost of capital concepts including debt, equity, and WACC
Understanding financial leverage and financing decisions in businesses
Basics of banking systems and modern digital banking operations
How commercial banks generate profits and manage financial risks
Understanding financial statements — Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow Statement
Financial ratio analysis to evaluate company performance and financial health
Identifying business risks and risk management techniques
Basics of portfolio management and risk–return trade-offs
Overview of insurance products and mutual fund categories
Introduction to financial modeling and forecasting techniques

Program Outcome

By the end of the internship, participants will develop a foundational understanding of financial analysis, banking operations, and investment decision-making used in real-world business environments.

Capital Structure Banking Ops Financial Statements Ratio Analysis Risk Management Portfolio Mgmt Mutual Funds Financial Modeling
