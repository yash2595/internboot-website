i PROGRAM OVERVIEW

Our Business Development Internship introduces participants to the strategies and tools used to identify growth opportunities, acquire new clients, and build lasting partnerships. You will learn how to conduct competitive market research, craft compelling pitches, manage a BD pipeline using CRM platforms, and execute go-to-market strategies that deliver measurable business impact.

Program Outcome

By the end of the internship, participants will gain foundational knowledge of business development strategies, client acquisition frameworks, and partnership management tools used by growth teams across industries. You will leave with a portfolio of real BD campaigns, hands-on CRM experience, and the confidence to pursue roles in business development, sales, strategy, or account management at leading organisations.
